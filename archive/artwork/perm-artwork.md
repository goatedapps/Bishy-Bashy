# Perm Mania artwork

Original character assets generated for this project with the built-in image-generation tool.
The final file is [perm-disco-sprites.png](perm-disco-sprites.png), copied unchanged from the generated PNG.
It is a 1536 × 1024 image with genuine transparency and a three-column, two-row sprite layout.
The first row contains the coral, mint, and blue dancers.
The bottom row contains the lavender dancer, an independently scalable hair silhouette, and a mirror ball.
CSS crops the cells, layers hair behind each dancer, and scales hair according to local or broadcast growth.
No original Bishi Bashi artwork or audio was reused.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production sprite sheet for an original playful browser party rhythm game called Perm Mania.
This is game artwork only, not a mockup or screenshot.
Create a 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a strict 3-column by 2-row equal square grid, six separate centered cutout assets, genuinely transparent background everywhere between the subjects, no grid lines.
Cells 1-4 contain four different friendly cartoon adult disco dancers.
Each is full body, front view, large head, short chibi proportions, little platform shoes and flared trousers, broad joyful facial expression and VERY SMALL close-cropped curly hair.
Keep every dancer's crown, face, shoulders and feet aligned at identical heights in their square cells, full body inside cell with generous clear margins.
Top row left: medium-brown-skinned man in coral disco jumpsuit.
Top row middle: fair-skinned woman in mint disco jumpsuit.
Top row right: dark-brown-skinned woman in sky blue disco jumpsuit.
Bottom row left: tan-skinned man in lavender disco jumpsuit.
All have one hand pointing up and the other on hip, energetic friendly original character designs, no references to celebrities.
Bottom row middle cell: ONE standalone big round fluffy afro hairstyle, very dark plum brown curls, full rounded silhouette, warm cocoa and subtle lavender highlights, a little flattened bottom to sit above a forehead, no face, no head, no body.
It should fill about 80 percent of the square cell's width, centered at the cell center with generous transparency around it.
This hair will be layered and scaled over the dancers using code.
Bottom row right cell: ONE cute mirror disco ball, silver and lilac mirrored tiles, small attached hanging loop, centered in its cell, no scene or lighting beams.
Style: chunky polished toy illustration, thick deep aubergine outlines, tactile hand-painted cartoon shading, candy coral #ff6568, mint #72dba0, sky #6bc8f4 and lavender #a583e8.
Crisp readable silhouettes at tiny sizes, consistent style throughout.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, interface, borders, logos, watermarks, background, checkerboard, floor, or shadows outside the silhouettes.
Keep every asset strictly inside its own cell without any overlap.
