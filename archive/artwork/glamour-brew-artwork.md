# Glamour Brew artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool generated the three retained PNG masters.
Regenerate runtime WebPs with `python scripts/optimize-images.py glamour-stages glamour-props bg-glamour-apothecary`.

The transformation atlas uses cells zero through ten in row-major order.

## glamour-stages.png

Transparent output.

> Create one transparent sprite atlas for a party game, landscape canvas ratio 4:3, with an EXACT regular grid of FOUR columns and THREE rows.
> Draw exactly twelve separate, equally sized chest-up head-and-shoulder sprites, one centered per cell with 8% fully transparent padding on all sides, reading left to right, top to bottom.
> Keep the same camera, eye line, centered head, head size, adult character identity, skin tone, chest crop, and front-facing pose at EVERY step.
> Art style: expressive thick-outline glossy cel-shaded Japanese arcade game, richly colored, high silhouette contrast.
> An adult female witch with purple hair is gradually transformed by beauty potions from a comically scruffy, hunched, wide-eyed, long hook-nosed witch with a floppy ragged hat and a chin wart into a radiant, elegant adult young woman in a glamorous purple dress, sparkling hair ornaments, and curled lavender hair.
> The transformation is a cartoon disguise shedding, not a new character.
> Each cell has a VISIBLY distinct upgrade and each row progresses much further.
> Top row stages 0–3: (0) big ragged pointed hat, very wild unkempt purple hair, hooked nose, crooked smile, wart, tattered gray-purple cloak; (1) brighter eyes and a small warm smile, hat still ragged; (2) wart disappears, hat straightens with a little pink ribbon, hair still frizzy; (3) nose is less hooked, hair begins to smooth, cloak collar gains color.
> Middle row stages 4–7: (4) shorter nose, clear expressive face, styled lavender side lock, mended purple cape; (5) little bloom on hat, smooth side-swept hair, blushing; (6) eyes sparkle and hair grows into clear curls, cape becomes a violet party dress, hat tilts playfully; (7) hat becomes a small violet fascinator, confident smile, gleaming brooch.
> Bottom row stages 8–11: (8) no pointed hat, jewelled hair clip, glamorous lavender curls and pink dress highlights; (9) elegant full curls, bright violet gown neckline and gold earrings; (10) fully transformed, beautiful smiling adult heroine, glossy voluminous lavender hair, gold star tiara and purple-magenta party dress with golden trim; (11) a matching delighted eyes-closed victory pose for the final heroine.
> Genuine transparent space everywhere outside the sprites.
> No cell borders, typography, trays, potion bottles, labels, background, ambient particles, or numbers.

## glamour-props.png

Transparent output.

> Transparent square arcade game prop atlas with EXACTLY TWO columns and TWO rows of equal cells, four isolated sprites centered with 10% transparent padding.
> Top left: red raspberry glass potion bottle with pale pink heart emblem, rounded heart-shaped body and gold stopper.
> Top right: bright green peridot glass potion bottle with pale mint leaf emblem, pear-shaped body and gold stopper.
> Bottom left: vivid royal blue glass potion bottle with pale white crescent-moon emblem, squat hexagonal bottle and gold stopper.
> Bottles all the same visual height and very easy to tell apart on a phone.
> Bottom right: one empty low flat horizontal round gold serving tray, slightly angled from above, short oval silhouette, no pedestal, no stand, no objects on top.
> Crisp thick line art, lively cel shading, stylized original Japanese arcade game props.
> No writing, no backgrounds, no grid lines, no extra objects.
