# Burst the Balloon artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool generated the two retained PNG masters.
Use `python scripts/optimize-images.py balloon-sprites bg-balloon-workshop` to regenerate their runtime WebPs.

## Balloon and pump

Master: `balloon-sprites.png`.

> Transparent game sprite sheet for a colorful hand-painted Japanese party arcade game.
> Landscape 2:1 canvas, two independent sprites exactly centered in equal left and right halves with transparent space between them, no overlap.
> LEFT HALF: one fully inflated glossy raspberry-red latex party balloon upright, rounded oval, tied at its bottom, no ribbon or string, complete silhouette.
> RIGHT HALF: one chunky mint-green hand air pump standing upright, body and cream T handle with a red grip, shown from straight on, no hose, complete silhouette.
> Large and clear at mobile scale, warm highlights, plum outlines, gouache cel illustration.
> Genuine transparent background.
> No words, no numbers, no labels, no decorative objects.
> Keep at least 8 percent transparent margin within each half.

## Party workshop

Master: `bg-balloon-workshop.png`.

> Wide landscape game backdrop for Burst the Balloon, a colorful hand-painted Japanese party arcade minigame.
> A cheerful party workshop with warm cream and sky-blue wallpaper, soft apricot wood workbench along the bottom fifth, little paper bunting along the upper corners, and distant muted shelves at the far left and right.
> Middle of frame quiet and low contrast for four big superimposed balloons, read well with narrow mobile crops.
> Gouache cel illustration, thin plum outlines, bright warm sunlight, cozy arcade aesthetic.
> No balloons, no pumps, no people, no lettering, no numbers.
