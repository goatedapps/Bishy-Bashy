# Open Sesame artwork

Both PNG masters were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.
The game renders numbers and the selection state over the transparent phone sprite.
Use `python scripts/optimize-images.py sesame-phone bg-sesame-exchange` to regenerate the delivery files.

## Rotary phone

Master: `sesame-phone.png`.

> Game sprite for Dokidoki Dash, a joyful hand-painted Japanese party arcade game.
> One complete coral-red and warm cream 1960s rotary desk telephone, straight overhead orthographic view, centered isolated cutout with actual transparent background.
> A horizontally resting curved handset across the top quarter of its enamel base; the prominent large circular brass and cream rotary wheel is perfectly face-on, centered horizontally at about 59% of total sprite height, about 63% of its width.
> Make the dial face blank and evenly colored so game-rendered number labels and selection can be layered precisely on top.
> Thin hand-drawn plum outlines, small warm highlights, vivid vintage toy, softly shaded cel illustration, bold compact silhouette legible at mobile size.
> Square composition, near full frame with small safe transparent margins.
> No digits, no letters, no branding, no background, no separate objects.

## Telephone exchange

Master: `bg-sesame-exchange.png`.

> Landscape background plate for a colorful Japanese party arcade game called Open Sesame.
> An illustrated vintage telephone exchange workshop with teal wallpaper and a warm wooden desk across the bottom, amber lamps and old switchboard sockets clustered at the far edges.
> Center is quiet, medium light and low contrast to make a superimposed coral rotary phone and cream digits easy to read.
> Hand-painted cartoon gouache shading, rounded forms, warm amber accents with petrol teal and plum outlines, welcoming retro arcade aesthetic.
> Wide landscape crop with details able to crop cleanly on phones.
> No telephones in the center, no characters, no words, no digits or logos.
