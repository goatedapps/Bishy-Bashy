# Dam Good Fit, Sushi Switchboard, and Mole Metro artwork

The PNGs in this directory are the retained masters.
The runtime scenes use their optimized WebP versions from the asset manifest.
Each cover is also used in the carousel, Arcade reel, Party picker, and game announcement.

## Dam Good Fit

Generate four edge-to-edge square panels in a 2×2 sheet with no gutters or text.
Use bright hand-painted chibi arcade illustration, deep plum outlines, and luminous gouache shading.
Place a frontal beaver dam with a plain wooden center and turquoise river in the top-left panel.
Place flat amber oak grain without perspective in the top-right panel.
Place a smiling beaver engineer in an orange hard hat carrying a jagged block beside a foaming dam in the bottom-left panel.
Place flowing white and aqua water in the bottom-right panel.
Crop the four panels separately for the dam, wood, cover, and water masters.

## Sushi Switchboard

Generate a square 2×2 sheet with no gutters or text in bright hand-painted chibi arcade style.
Place a frontal sushi bar with rice-paper lanterns, teal tiles, and an open wooden counter in the top-left panel.
Place a cheerful tanuki sushi chef in a white headband holding a plate of sushi in the top-right panel.
Use the two top panels for the counter and cover masters.

Generate a separate equal-cell 3×3 sprite sheet on solid white with no grid lines, text, plates, or outside shadows.
Center and isolate one food item per cell with a deep plum outline and a consistent three-quarter view.
In row one, draw salmon nigiri, tuna nigiri, and shrimp nigiri.
In row two, draw egg nigiri, glazed eel nigiri, and cucumber maki.
In row three, draw avocado maki, salmon roe gunkan, and an inari tofu pouch.

## Mole Metro

Generate a square 2×2 sheet with no gutters or text in bright hand-painted chibi arcade style with dark plum outlines.
In the top-left panel, draw an overhead empty mine cavern with flat muted violet earth and lanterns and mineral rocks near the edges.
In the top-right panel, isolate an upward-facing orange and yellow mining locomotive with a lilac roof and lights against pure white.
In the bottom-left panel, draw a chibi mole conductor in a navy cap leaning out of a locomotive amid glowing emerald crystals.
Crop the mine, locomotive, and cover panels separately, and remove the white around the locomotive for the sprite master.
